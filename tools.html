  • Google Maps Contractor License Check. Check contractors scraped from Google Maps against official state license registers (CA, AZ, WA, FL, TX and more). Each business gets license number, type, status, expiry and the register phone, matched by phone or name. Pay only for licenses found.

Export a whole trade (roofing, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, pools) or verify a list of names or licence numbers. Status, expiry date, address, county. No phone: Florida does not publish it. Official register, measured coverage
  • US Active Contractors - Permits + License, Phone. Contractors who pulled a building permit in the last 90 days in Seattle, Orlando, Cincinnati or Marin County, matched to their state licence: status, expiry date, phone. One row per contractor with permit count, total job value and lead score. 74 % matched in Seattle, measured.
  • US Building Permits - Contractor Leads with Delta Mode. Call the contractor the week the permit is issued. Permits from 17 US city registers via official open-data APIs: contractor, license and phone, job value, GPS, trade and a lead score. New York and Mesa: is the license still active? Delta mode charges only new permits.

How do I find Airbnb hosts, new listings or short-term rental licenses?

  • Airbnb Multi-Listing Hosts by City. Find the hosts running more than one Airbnb listing in a city. One row per host with portfolio size, superhost status, average rating, total reviews and median price. The listing data is public; the portfolio is not, and that is the product.
  • Airbnb Reviews Scraper by City or Listing, Translated. Airbnb reviews for any listing URL, or for the top listings of any city: rating, date, text with English translation, host reply, stay length, reviewer first name and home town, plus Airbnb's topic summaries per listing. Filter by stars, date or Airbnb's review search. Feed mode: only new reviews.
  • Airbnb New Listings & Price Drops by City. Airbnb listings for any city with two signals other scrapers skip: which listings are new (no reviews yet, or not seen in your last run) and which dropped their price (Airbnb's own 'originally' price, plus the change since your last run). Host name and Superhost on request. Schedule it for a feed.
