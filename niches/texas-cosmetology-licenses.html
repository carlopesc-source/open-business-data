How many licensed cosmetologists and salons are there in Texas?

Texas has 382,479 current cosmetology, barber, esthetician and manicurist licenses, 14,205 of them for establishments and schools, according to the TDLR register.

How do I look up a cosmetology license in Texas?

The Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR) publishes every license with its number, type and expiry date on data.texas.gov.
